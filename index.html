HOW TO PLAY HUISWEEPER

Pure classic Minesweeper logic reimagined with photorealistic Indian sweets.

1 Ghewar Pip Exactly 1 adjacent Soan Papdi hazard box touches this tile.
Dice Pip Layouts Counts 1 to 8 arrange ghewars in standard dice patterns. Count the sweets!
Spider-Tracer Flag Right-click or long-press suspected Soan Papdi boxes to lock them safely.
Soan Papdi Hazard Avoid clicking directly! If clicked, dry flaky besan explodes across the board.
1
Click to Reveal: Click or tap any covered tile. The first click is always guaranteed 100% safe.
2
Count the Ghewars: Instead of plain numerals, delicious Rajasthani Ghewars are arranged in dice patterns representing the exact count of neighboring hazards.
3
Chording Shortcut: Once you have flagged all adjacent hazards for an open tile, clicking that open tile uncovers all remaining unflagged neighbors instantly.
