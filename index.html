GAME RULES AND MECHANICS

Huisweeper operates with classic Minesweeper logic using traditional sweets:

Golden Ghewar (Safe Count)
Safe tiles. Count the number of Ghewars visible on the revealed tile (arranged in dice patterns from 1 to 8) to know exactly how many adjacent Soan Papdi boxes are touching that cell.
Dangerous Soan Papdi
The hazard mine. Detonates if clicked. Flaky, dry, and regifted year after year. Flag all suspected boxes to clear the board.
Hazard Flags
Mark suspected Soan Papdi locations. Right-click on desktop, long-press on touch screens, or toggle the Flag Mode button.
Tile Chording
Once you flag all adjacent hazards for an open numbered tile, clicking the tile again will instantly uncover all remaining unflagged adjacent safe tiles.
