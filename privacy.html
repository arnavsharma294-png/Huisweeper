Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

1. Overview

Huisweeper operates as an entirely client-side web application. We believe in strict digital privacy. We do not track, profile, monetize, or harvest user data. All gameplay calculations occur locally in your web browser.

2. Information Collection

Huisweeper does not collect personal identification information. We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, payment methods, or phone numbers.

  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not deploy third-party trackers, pixel tags, or behavioral analytics software.
  • No Advertising Networks: The game is completely free of advertisements and data-broker integrations.
  • No Server-Side Logging: Gameplay actions and outcomes remain exclusively on your device.

3. Browser Local Storage

The game utilizes HTML5 LocalStorage strictly to preserve local client-side preferences. Specifically:

  • huisweeper_muted: Stores your preference regarding audio sound effects (true or false).

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Fonts and Assets

Font stylesheets are delivered through Google Fonts infrastructure. No personal user data is collected by us during asset delivery.

5. Policy Changes

Should this privacy policy be updated in the future, revisions will be published directly to this page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact

For questions or technical inquiries regarding Huisweeper privacy practices, contact us at privacy@huisweeper.com.
